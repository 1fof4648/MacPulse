# 🔋 MacPulse - Smarter Battery Life, Automatically

---

## 🚀 What Is MacPulse?

MacPulse is a small, smart helper for your Mac that watches how your battery **actually behaves**—not just a simple percentage—and predicts when it will run out. Instead of waiting until you hit a low number like 20%, MacPulse looks at your real usage patterns and turns on **Low Power Mode** exactly when you really need it.

Think of it like this: your Mac's battery meter is a clock, and MacPulse is the person who knows when you'll actually be late.

---

## 🎯 Why You'll Love It

- ⚡ **Adaptive Battery Predictions**  
  MacPulse doesn't guess. It uses a clever math trick called a **Kalman filter** to learn your battery's real drain rate and predict the future with 95% confidence.

- 🔕 **No More Surprise Shutdowns**  
  Instead of waiting until your battery is nearly dead, MacPulse steps in early so your Mac keeps working smoothly.

- 🧘 **Set It and Forget It**  
  Once installed, MacPulse runs quietly in your menu bar. No settings to fiddle with, no pop-ups, no clutter.

- 🍎 **Made for macOS 27**  
  Built with Apple's latest design language—**Liquid Glass**—so it looks right at home on your Mac.

## 🛠️ How to Run MacPulse (Step-by-Step)

1. **Find the downloaded ZIP file** — It usually lands in your **Downloads** folder.
2. **Double-click the ZIP file** — Your Mac will automatically unzip it into a folder called `MacPulse-master` (or similar).
3. **Open that folder** — You'll see a file named `MacPulse` (it might look like a small app icon).
4. **Double-click `MacPulse`** — macOS might ask for permission the first time. Just click **"Open"**.
5. **Look at your menu bar** — The little battery icon at the top right of your screen. MacPulse will appear right there.

That's it. MacPulse is now running.
